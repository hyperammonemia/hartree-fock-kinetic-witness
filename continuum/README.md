# The fixed Hamiltonian and its proof obligations

`ContinuumHamiltonian.FixedRadialReversal` states the physical question for the explicit field in this repository. Both electrons range over three-dimensional Euclidean space. Each of the four complex spin components has six square-integrable weak partial derivatives. The exact energy is minimized over normalized antisymmetric states of finite form energy. The Hartree–Fock minimization tests all admissible determinants of two orthonormal complex spin orbitals. The target requires a radial closed shell, uniqueness of the minimizing determinant up to phase, and a strict inequality between the two integrals of squared weak gradients. The target remains open in Lean. [Physical definitions](ContinuumHamiltonian.lean) · [Fixed external potential](../FinitePotential.lean) · [Analytic construction](../continuum-witness.md)

| Result | Hypotheses that matter for the physical question |
|---|---|
| The radial Gaussian integral in the second Hartree moment is exactly (2/3). | Its interpretation as the Newton potential of the physical Gaussian uses the Coulomb convolution identity. [Integral](GaussianCoulomb.lean) |
| The explicit exterior barrier obeys the radial oscillator differential inequality. A weighted weak maximum principle orders the exterior profiles. | The reference state's marginal must satisfy the required distributional inequality and boundary condition. [Barrier](RadialBarrier.lean) · [Comparison](WeakRadial.lean) |
| `RadialProbe.spatial_margin` gives the strict positive margin for the integral on three-dimensional space with the fixed cutoff and frequency. | It takes an almost-everywhere bound on the square of the radial marginal and an integrability condition. It includes the angular factor and the two-electron normalization. [Spatial integral](RadialProbe.lean) |
| The finite-frequency squared-gradient error is bounded by (54b) for the sum of the two electron contributions. | Its vector fields must be the product gradients of the physical states under comparison. The separate integral calculation bounds the normalization of the weighted wavefunction. [Gradient estimate](FiniteKinetic.lean) · [Normalization](WeightNormalization.lean) |
| The abstract form gap transfers a Gaussian-complement bound to a variational minimizer. The concrete mass and positive-energy measures are defined on the two-electron configuration space. | Applying these results to the same minimizing wavefunction requires the physical form-domain and energy identifications. [Variational theorem](UnitVariational.lean) · [Configuration-space measures](RadialConfiguration.lean) |

Run `python tools/fetch_cache.py` and `lake build ContinuumResults` from the repository root. A proposed final proof can be tested with `python tools/check_physical_theorem.py MODULE DECLARATION`. The checker assigns the declaration to the exact target type and inspects the resulting theorem's axioms. A conditional implication or the definition of the target cannot pass that check. [Target checker](../tools/check_physical_theorem.py)
