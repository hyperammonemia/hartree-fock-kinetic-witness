# The fixed Hamiltonian and its proof obligations

`ContinuumHamiltonian.FixedRadialReversal` states the physical question for the explicit field in this repository. Both electrons range over three-dimensional Euclidean space. Each of the four complex spin components has six square-integrable weak partial derivatives. The exact energy is minimized over normalized antisymmetric states of finite form energy. The Hartree–Fock minimization tests all admissible determinants of two orthonormal complex spin orbitals. The target requires a radial closed shell, uniqueness of the minimizing determinant up to phase, and a strict inequality between the two integrals of squared weak gradients. The target remains open in Lean. [Physical definitions](ContinuumHamiltonian.lean) · [Fixed external potential](../FinitePotential.lean) · [Analytic construction](../continuum-witness.md)
